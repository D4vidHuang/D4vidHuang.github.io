## Visitor services

- CounterAPI supplies the aggregate unique-visitor total using an anonymized visitor hash: https://counterapi.com/
- Whos.amung.us supplies the optional live location map. Its script is loaded only after a visitor presses the map button: https://whos.amung.us/legal/privacy/

The two services report different populations; the opt-in location map is not presented as a geographic breakdown of the aggregate total.
